How to Work Together

Three ways in, depending on where you are.

Most brand teams don't need a full content operation on day one. They need to know whether the opportunity is real for them, and what it would take. Start where you are.

The Strategy Session

A half day working through your specific situation. Where your audience actually spends attention, which content formats fit your brand and budget, and what the first twelve months would realistically look like.

You leave with a written set of recommendations and a prioritized list of what to do first. Not a proposal. A plan you can execute with or without Jeff.

Best for teams who know branded entertainment matters but haven't scoped what it means for them.

A four to six week engagement that produces the plan your team builds against.

Jeff assesses what you have, identifies the content formats and distribution paths that fit your audience and your resources, and builds the measurement framework so you can prove it's working before the next budget cycle.

Delivered as a working document your team can act on, plus a session to walk your stakeholders through it.
