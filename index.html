Your Guide

The Playbook Is to Become a Media Company.

Every brand that owns its audience got there the same way. It stopped buying attention and started operating like a publisher — a slate of programming, a distribution strategy, and the measurement discipline to prove it's working. That's an operating model.

Most brand leaders can see the destination and have no map to it: a team built to make campaigns, no platform relationships, and a board that wants returns in two quarters.

Jeff has spent his career on the inside of the media business — working with Warner Bros, Disney, Paramount, Comcast/NBCU, and Amazon Studios, and 15+ years in audience measurement at Nielsen and Comscore. He knows how programming gets made, distributed, and counted.

He's built that same function on the brand side as fractional Chief Content Officer and executive producer.

The model already exists. The work is building it for your brand.
